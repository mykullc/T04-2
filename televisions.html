Television Energy Consumption

Televisions are common appliances in Australian households. The amount of energy a television uses can depend on its screen size, viewing time and energy efficiency.

Screen Size

Larger television screens generally use more energy than smaller screens. Choosing a suitable screen size can help reduce electricity consumption.

Viewing Time

A television uses more energy when it is switched on for longer periods. Turning the television off when it is not being watched can help save energy.

Energy Efficiency

Energy-efficient televisions use less electricity during operation. Australian energy labels can help consumers compare the efficiency of different television models.
