Appliance Energy Consumption

Appliances use energy every day in Australian households. Understanding how much energy appliances consume can help people reduce electricity use, save money and make more environmentally friendly choices.

Energy Usage

Energy usage describes the amount of electricity an appliance needs to operate. Appliances that are used for long periods may consume more energy.

Energy Efficiency

Energy-efficient appliances use less electricity while still performing their main functions. Choosing efficient appliances can help reduce household energy consumption.

Australian Households

Australian households use a variety of appliances, including televisions, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners. Learning about their energy use can support better decisions.
