About Us

This website provides information about appliance energy consumption and helps users understand how household appliances use electricity.

University Project

This website was created as part of a university project. The project explores how information about energy consumption can be presented in a clear and useful way.

Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

The website focuses on the energy consumption of appliances used in Australian households. Understanding appliance energy use can help people make informed decisions about electricity usage.

Data Visualisation

The website will use data visualisation to explore television energy consumption. Charts and other visual information can make energy data easier to understand and compare.
